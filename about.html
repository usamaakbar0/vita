Your Career, Our Commitment

Founders

Nichole

Founder, Vita Recruitment Ltd.

With more than a decade of international experience, Nichole has dedicated her career to unlocking human potential. Her nursing, business development, and marketing background broadened her expertise, culminating in the launch of Vita Recruitment Limited — a recruitment model built to specialise in connecting skilled professionals from diverse workforces with global opportunities. As one of the Founders of Vita, she envisions a more adaptable, efficient, and personal lens to recruitment that forges lasting relationships with clients, bringing a unique, genuine, and impactful global perspective to the recruitment landscape.

Kina

Co-Founder, Vita Recruitment Limited

Kina is one of the Founders of Vita Recruitment, bringing a rare and formidable blend of public service discipline, entrepreneurial grit, and a deep commercial intuition to the recruitment industry. A successful entrepreneur with a Business Management degree, she possesses an instinctive expertise of what it takes to build, sustain and grow a business — an insight elevated by her tenure as a Personal Assistant to the Office of the President, where she operated at the highest echelons of governance, navigated complex logistics, and delivered strategic support under immense pressure, forging dedication to excellence, precision, and integrity. This rare vantage defines her unique approach to recruitment: decoding corporate culture, assessing leadership potential, and aligning skilled professionals with the right business — an unparalleled strategy that connects to the company’s commitment to a diverse and inclusive workforce, ensuring that every placement creates a long-term, authentic global partnership.
